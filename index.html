HOW IT WORKS

Calculation sequence

  1. Select your loan category.
  2. Enter principal, annual rate and tenure.
  3. Choose Years or Months. The numeric field and slider stay synchronized.
  4. Press Calculate EMI to generate the repayment summary.
DATA TRANSPARENCY

What is estimated?

This calculator uses a standard monthly reducing-balance EMI formula. Actual lender schedules may vary because of disbursement timing, floating-rate resets, daily interest, fees and rounding.
